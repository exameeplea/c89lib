# What c89lib is?
This is a collection of header-only libraries, made up with <br>
comprehensive functionality upon one idea, task, need. <br>
<br>
Every library you can meet in c89lib, an extension. By that <br>
I mean its flexible, stickiness, unobtrusiveness. It can perfect <br>
fit your project without any efforts. It will be fully transparent, <br>
expanding existing C89-compat libraries in their natural manner. <br>
<br>

# Why exactly C89?
I had worked on similiar idea for C99, it was called xst99c, <br>
which meaning is <Extensions 99 for C> and 1999's C standard. <br>
<br>
But then I realized how much I want these extensions work on <br>
ancient systems, old compilers. I don't mean anything against C99, <br>
you can use c89lib even in C23 standard, thanksfully of compatibility. <br>
Someday I saw in C99 implicitly sugar-useless magic, ruins discipline. <br>
And yet, also I was inspired by such libraries, like libharu, libcurl, etc. <br>
<br>

# Why MIT, not MPL?
I had a thought about this. MPL is good license, which guarantees that all <br>
improves will be given back to the open-source community. But.. I don't <br>
want to ruin the essence, the feeling of the developer invention. Just imagine <br>
someone had improved these libraries in better way, and them will have full right <br>
to keep these changes as their individual <programmer's kit>. Even enterprise players. <br>
Also, I don't asking anyone to help me with. Just use it, if you want. These are <br>
instruments created by me in that form, version, feeling, I had initially did. It's me, <br>
not others. So because of that, to clear the paths, I had picked MIT license instead MPL. <br>
<br>

# And... If you want to help anyway,
When I was developing the libraries, I knew that there are stuff I can't test fully. <br>
So I had left in the whole source texts something called <#errno wizards>. It will help you <br>
to implement my libraries if something goes wrong on your yet unsupported platform in comfortable <br>
way. So, feel free to modify the texts, and if you want, publish these useful changes to others!
